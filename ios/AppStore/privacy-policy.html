XENO ZIRAN SIMULATOR — Privacy Policy

iOS and iPadOS app · effective 6 October 2026

The interactive simulator runs locally on the device and does not transmit simulation state, imported files, or usage data. Network access occurs only when the user explicitly opens external research resources in Safari.

Data the app collects

None. The app contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party SDKs, and it does not create accounts.

Simulation state

Sessions are held in memory on your device while the app runs. They are not sent anywhere and are not kept by the app after it is closed.

Files you import, save or export

Files you load are read on your device. Saved sessions and exports are written only where you send them through the system share sheet, by your own action.

External links

The app links to the project's public research-results website. The link opens in your browser only when you tap it; your browser then connects to that website.

Apple services

Apple processes standard data related to App Store and TestFlight distribution (for example downloads, crash reports or feedback you choose to share) under Apple's own privacy policy, outside the app's control.
