XENO ZIRAN SIMULATOR

An environment for exploring how differences propagate across configurations under changes in scale, topology, memory, coupling, observation, history, boundary and intervention. It does not fix the variables, state space, observer or ontology in advance. Its operations fall into distinct regions: an experimental runtime, research results produced on it, and the Philosophical Runtime for philosophical analysis.

This public deployment is the simulator's read-only research-result layer: resolution matrix, hidden differences, scale penetration, reasoning structures, disconnection, long-run compression, runtime reduction, reason non-redundancy, phase timeline and primitive inspector, read from the experiment result files. Nothing is simulated on this site; the interactive runtime runs on a local server. The published result views are experimental and analytical outputs and should not be interpreted as real-world forecasts or predictive claims.
