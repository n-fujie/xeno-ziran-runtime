# Superseded measurements (kept)

`G_v0_push_during_movement.json` — the first version of Experiment G (12 seeds, all fourteen principles, ablation), in which the force
ladder was applied **while the body was moving towards the cue target**. Differences in body state at the moment of the push then appeared
as differences in what the push did: nine of fourteen principles showed a "transfer", most through `peak_dev_forward` and
`correction_latency`. The ladder was changed to push the body **at rest** (identical state in every condition); the transfers listed
in the final records are from the state-matched version. The change was made to remove a confound, was decided before the final seeds were
evaluated, and the first result is kept here, not deleted. (Its record carries its own digest and can be imported unchanged.)
